## Implementation choices to review

These are choices made while building to the guide where the guide is silent on a detail. They are listed so a reviewer can accept or change them; none is presented as the guide's rule.

- **Offer-mail approval** reuses the `admin.manage` permission (held only by the Main User) and the existing `email_log` table: a prepared mail is a committed `queued` row, because a mail being delivered is queued and finished inside one transaction.
- **Joining reminder time**: the guide gives a joining date, not a time. The reminder is 24 hours before the stored joining deadline; with a date only, the deadline is the start of that day in Asia/Colombo (the project's date-only convention). No clock time was invented.
- **HR permissions are taken literally**: view candidates and interviews, add/edit candidates, enter results, send mail, manage company accounts. HR does **not** manage offers or the employee lifecycle (the guide lists neither). Intern / Dev holds the operating permissions but no salary and no administration.
- **Salary for Intern / Dev**: "dummy salary only for development" is met by test data entered by the Main User; no salary permission is granted to Intern / Dev.
- **Past deadlines**: phase reminders whose time has passed are still sent (HR needs the gate decision); interview and joining reminders for a time already past are not created.
- **Duplicate rule for scheduled mails** is per reminder (one phase, interview or offer), since the same "phase-end reminder" template legitimately goes out for each phase. One-off mails keep "once per candidate and template unless Resend".
- **The CV reminder** uses the template type `cv_pending_reminder`; the guide describes the reminder but names no separate template.
- **A reminder with no possible recipient** is kept as a failed row with its reason (visible, never sent) instead of silently not existing.
- **Team Lead scope** is by the Team Lead row linked to their login; a Team Lead without that link sees no candidates.
