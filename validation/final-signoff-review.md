### Junior end date

- **Implemented** on the project owner's decision (recorded as an implementation decision, **not** formal HR sign-off): junior starts the day after probation (or its extension) finishes and ends `EDATE(start, junior_months) - 1 day`, using the existing editable `junior_months` setting. No migration and no new setting.
- **Guide's worked example** (join 2 Nov 2026) is reproduced end to end by an automated test: evaluation 2 to 8 Nov 2026, probation 9 Nov 2026 to 8 Feb 2027, junior 9 Feb to 8 Nov 2027, Senior from 9 Nov 2027.
- **Where:** the Probation Pass creates the junior row with its end date, using `juniorPhaseEnd`; the existing reminder code then creates the `junior_end` notification at 17:30 Asia/Colombo, sent 24 hours before, with the existing one-pending-reminder-per-phase rule. A Junior Pass uses the stored end and starts Senior the next day.
- **Existing records:** nothing is backfilled or recalculated. A junior phase created earlier keeps its empty end date and HR still enters it at the Pass.
- **Still open:** HR's own confirmation of the reading. If HR chooses "from joining", `juniorPhaseEnd` already supports that basis (unused) and the change is one argument.

### HR and offers (permission review)

- **Official guide:** HR "adds and edits candidates, results, sends mails"; section 7 says HR enters and updates everything, including **step 4, the Offer Tracker (offer, joining date, attendance)**, and gate-decision buttons go to HR and the manager. The Main User approves offer mails; salary is the Main User's alone.
- **Current role map (migration 009):** HR has `candidates.view`, `candidates.edit`, `interviews.manage`, `notifications.manage`, `company_accounts.manage`. HR has **no** `offers.manage` and **no** `employees.manage`. Only Intern/Dev and the Main User hold them.
- **Effect:** HR can open Prepare Offer but cannot save an offer, and cannot record attendance or gate decisions.
- **Smallest correction:** give HR `offers.manage` (and, if HR records gate decisions, `employees.manage`) by a data-only migration. Salary is unaffected: `salary.view` and `salary.edit` stay with the Main User and the offer endpoints carry no salary (tested).
- **Not applied:** the guide does not say in one sentence which of the two HR may do, so this needs your approval.

### Email readiness

- **Verified in development:** templates, preview, edit, Resend, one-click and Auto modes, Main User approval, duplicate prevention, the 15-minute job, the Monday report, the calendar attachment and the email log, all with fictional addresses and a spy or development adapter.
- **Missing for real delivery:** a production adapter in `mailService.js` (SMTP or provider API); mailbox credentials supplied through the environment; none of the configuration variables exists yet; active templates and recipient addresses; a real test to company addresses; a client check of the `.ics`.
